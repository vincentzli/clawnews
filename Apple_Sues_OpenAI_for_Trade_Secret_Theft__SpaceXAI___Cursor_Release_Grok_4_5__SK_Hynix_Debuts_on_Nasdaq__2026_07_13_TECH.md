# Apple Sues OpenAI for Trade Secret Theft; SpaceXAI & Cursor Release Grok 4.5; SK Hynix Debuts on Nasdaq

### **Apple Files High-Stakes Federal Lawsuit Against OpenAI Over Alleged Trade Secret Theft**
[Read More](news/Silicon_Heist_Inside_Apple’s_Federal_Trade_Secret_Lawsuit_Against_OpenAI_and_the_Battle_for_AI-Native_Hardware.md)
On July 10, 2026, Apple filed a major federal lawsuit in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California against OpenAI, its affiliate io Products (acquired by OpenAI for $6.5 billion), and former Apple employees Tang Tan and Chang Liu. The complaint alleges a systematic, coordinated campaign of trade secret theft designed to accelerate OpenAI's consumer AI hardware ambitions. Tang Tan, former Apple VP of Product Design and now OpenAI’s Chief Hardware Officer, is accused of orchestrating "show and tell" interviews where candidates still employed at Apple were pressured to bring physical components, logic boards, and batteries to display. Additionally, former Apple electrical engineer Chang Liu is accused of keeping an Apple-issued laptop after joining OpenAI in January 2026, exploiting a zero-day authentication bypass to download dozens of highly sensitive documents detailing hardware design, manufacturing specs, and internal supply chain logistics. With over 400 former Apple employees now working at OpenAI, the legal fight threatens to disrupt the once-lucrative partnership that integrated ChatGPT into Apple's on-device Siri architecture. Apple is seeking substantial monetary damages and an injunction to halt OpenAI's utilization of the stolen technologies. In response, OpenAI denied the claims, asserting that it has "no interest in other companies' trade secrets" and remains dedicated to its proprietary breakthroughs. The debate on social media has centered on the ethics of AI hiring practices and the intense corporate race to dominate local, on-device consumer AI hardware.

### **SpaceXAI and Cursor Release Grok 4.5 Amid Benchmark Contamination Controversy**
[Read More](news/The_Grok_4.5_Contamination_Scandal_Inside_SpaceX’s_60B_Cursor_Bet_and_the_Fragile_Illusion_of_Frontier_AI_Benchmarks.md)
On July 8, 2026, SpaceXAI (recently rebranded from xAI to unify Elon Musk's AI and space ventures) and Cursor (the AI coding platform acquired by SpaceX for $60 billion in June 2026) officially released Grok 4.5. Developed as the first model jointly built by the two companies, Grok 4.5 was designed to be an "Opus-class" model focused on software engineering, data science, and complex multi-agent workflows, claiming throughput of 80 tokens per second (TPS) and aggressive pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $6 per million output tokens. However, the launch immediately ignited a fierce controversy over benchmark integrity. Independent researchers and developers on X.com and Reddit pointed out significant "label-level contamination" where Cursor's private testing codebase, used to evaluate the model, was accidentally included in Grok 4.5's training data. This overfitting issue cast doubt on the model's reported superiority on coding benchmarks. Critics argue that vendor leaderboards are increasingly compromised by data contamination, while defenders argue that Grok 4.5's raw execution speeds and Cursor integration provide genuine economic advantages for enterprise workloads. The launch is also seen as a precursor to "Sand," an upcoming general-purpose AI agent designed to automate broader office tasks and directly compete with Anthropic's Claude Cowork.

### **SK Hynix Debuts on Nasdaq After Historic $26.5 Billion ADR Listing to Scale HBM Production**
[Read More](news/Untitled_17.md)
Highlighting the insatiable demand for AI hardware, South Korean memory giant SK Hynix listed its American Depositary Receipts (ADRs) on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. Trading under the temporary ticker SKHYV on July 10, 2026, before transitioning to its permanent ticker SKHY on July 13, 2026, the offering raised approximately $26.5 billion. This transaction represents the largest first-time U.S. listing by a foreign company in history. SK Hynix, which commands a dominant share of the global High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM) market crucial for AI accelerators, plans to use the massive proceeds to construct new fabs and purchase advanced lithography equipment. The listing coincided with a record-setting July where global memory sales reached $74.6 billion, up 31.7% from June. Although the stock experienced some "sell-the-news" profit-taking on the Seoul exchange, analysts agree that the fundamental market positioning of SK Hynix remains rock-solid as the industry demands denser, faster HBM to feed next-generation training clusters.

### **FDA Approves Eisai and Biogen's Leqembi Iqlik Once-Weekly Subcutaneous Autoinjector**
[Read More](news/At-Home_Alzheimer’s_Therapy_Is_Here_The_Tech,_Pharmacokinetics,_and_Medicare_Part_D_Arbitrage_of_Subcutaneous_Leqembi_Iqlik.md)
On July 13, 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) approved a supplemental Biologics License Application (sBLA) for Leqembi Iqlik (lecanemab-irmb), a once-weekly subcutaneous autoinjector. Developed by Eisai and Biogen, this new formulation allows patients to begin their early Alzheimer’s disease treatment directly at home with a weekly dose of 500 mg (administered via two 250 mg injections taking 15 seconds each), bypassing the need for initial biweekly intravenous (IV) clinic infusions. It is also approved for maintenance dosing at 360 mg once weekly after 18 months of treatment. This represents a massive quality-of-life improvement for patients, reducing clinic visit burdens and healthcare costs. The companies plan to launch Leqembi Iqlik in the United States in late August 2026, marking a significant milestone in patient-centered neurodegenerative therapies.

### **Global Robotics Consortiums Sign Barcelona Declaration to Standardize Policy and Automation**
On July 13, 2026, four of the world’s leading automation advocacy organizations—the International Federation of Robotics (IFR), the U.S.-based Association for Advancing Automation (A3), Germany’s VDMA Robotics + Automation, and Spain’s AER Automation—signed the "Barcelona Declaration on Robotics and Automation 2026." Representing over 3,000 companies and research institutions, the signatories established a permanent framework urging governments to adopt national robotics strategies, improve tax conditions for private investment, and utilize assistive care robotics in public sectors. Crucially, the declaration emphasizes communicating the evidence-based workforce impacts of automation to counter job loss anxiety, while promoting the integration of robotics into standard educational curricula to prepare future generations for an automated economy.

### **ESO's Extremely Large Telescope Achieves First Structure Rotation Milestone in Chile**
The European Southern Observatory's (ESO) Extremely Large Telescope (ELT), currently under construction on Cerro Armazones in the Chilean Atacama Desert, achieved a monumental engineering milestone on July 13, 2026. For the first time, engineers successfully rotated the massive 3,500-metric-ton steel structure around its vertical azimuth axis. The dome structure, resting on a layer of oil just 80 microns thick to facilitate smooth motion, was rotated manually by a few centimeters before using auxiliary electric motors for a full rotation. This test verifies the precision alignment and mechanics of the telescope’s structural motion systems. Once scientific instruments and the primary mirror are fully integrated, the telescope will weigh approximately 4,600 metric tons, aiming for its "first light" target in 2029 to provide unprecedented observations of the deep universe.
