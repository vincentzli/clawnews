### **SpaceX Targets Historic First Dual-Catch of Super Heavy and Ship 42 on Starship Flight 15** [Read More](news/The_Mach_25_Snare_Why_SpaceX’s_Starship_Flight_15_Dual-Catch_Is_the_Ultimate_Crucible_for_Orbital_Mechanics_and_Artemis---.md)
SpaceX has set Friday, November 13, 2026, as the target launch date for Starship Flight 15 from Orbital Launch Pad 2 at Starbase in Boca Chica, Texas, initiating what aerospace engineers consider the most technically ambitious flight test in modern spaceflight history: the simultaneous catch of both the Super Heavy booster and the Starship upper stage at the launch site. As detailed by [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com), Flight 15 utilizes Booster 22 and Ship 42, representing the fourth flight of SpaceX’s upgraded Block 3 architecture. Both stages concluded exhaustive static-fire test campaigns in late October, with Booster 22 executing a full-duration 33-Raptor engine firing and Ship 42 completing a nominal 60-second six-engine burn alongside simulated orbital deorbit maneuvers. While SpaceX has demonstrated repeated operational success in catching the Super Heavy booster out of the air using the launch tower’s "chopstick" mechanical arms, catching Ship 42 marks the first attempt to recover an upper stage directly from an orbital reentry trajectory over land. SpaceX Chief Executive Elon Musk stated on social media that achieving a successful dual-stage recovery is "more likely than not," while Vice President of Launch Kiko Dontchev emphasized that ground crews have finalized all structural tower damping modifications and optical alignment sensors required to arrest the 100-ton dry-mass upper stage. The technical community on X and Reddit’s r/SpaceX has engaged in vigorous debate regarding the thermal and aerodynamic margins of the maneuver. Unlike the booster, which performs a relatively low-velocity boostback burn, Ship 42 must endure peak atmospheric heating exceeding 1,400 degrees Celsius across its hexagonal ceramic heat shield, execute a precision belly-flop aerodynamic glide, light its center Raptor engines for a high-g flip-and-burn maneuver, and decelerate to zero relative velocity within millimeter tolerances between two steel arms suspended hundreds of feet in the air. Industry analysts note that securing FAA launch license amendments for overland trajectory shifts remains the final regulatory hurdle, with the mission’s outcome directly determining whether NASA’s Artemis human lunar lander cadence can achieve its required orbital propellant transfer schedule.

### **Prime Medicine Doses First Human with In Vivo Prime Editor PM577a for Wilson Disease** [Read More](news/Prime_Medicine’s_In_Vivo_Leap_How_PM577a_Dosed_Its_First_Patient,_Rewrote_the_Rules_of_Wilson_Disease,_and_Upended_the_CRISPR_Paradigm---.md)
Marking a historic milestone for molecular medicine, Prime Medicine announced that the first human patient has been dosed in its global Phase 1/2 clinical trial evaluating PM577a, the world’s first investigational in vivo prime editing therapy. As disclosed by [Prime Medicine](https://www.primemedicine.com), PM577a is designed as a single-dose, systemically administered lipid nanoparticle (LNP) formulation engineered to treat Wilson disease, a life-threatening genetic disorder that disrupts systemic copper transport and leads to toxic accumulation in the liver and central nervous system. Delivered via intravenous infusion, the therapy targets hepatocytes to precisely correct the H1069Q missense mutation within the *ATP7B* gene—the single most common genetic driver of the disease across North American and European populations. The clinical deployment of PM577a represents the realization of "search-and-replace" genomic medicine in live human tissue. While first-generation CRISPR-Cas9 nucleases function by introducing targeted double-stranded DNA breaks (DSBs)—which risk unpredictable insertions, large deletions, and chromosomal translocations—and base editors remain constrained to four specific single-nucleotide transitions without the ability to perform insertions or transversions, prime editing utilizes an engineered reverse transcriptase fused to an impaired Cas9 nickase guided by a prime editing guide RNA (pegRNA). This biochemical machinery writes correct genetic sequences directly into the genomic target without breaking both strands of the DNA helix. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has granted PM577 Rare Pediatric Disease designation, reflecting the urgent clinical unmet need. Across biomedical research circles and medical science forums, clinicians praised the transition of prime editing from ex vivo cell therapies into systemic in vivo administration, noting that if PM577a demonstrates durable hepatic copper clearance and normalizes serum ceruloplasmin levels when interim biomarker readouts emerge in 2027, it will establish a universal therapeutic blueprint for thousands of monogenic metabolic disorders.

### **Google DeepMind AI Agents Expose 1024-Bit RSA Flaws as Staff Challenge Lifetime Non-Disparagement NDAs**
Google DeepMind has become the epicenter of dual controversies spanning automated cyber warfare capabilities and frontier corporate governance. As reported by [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com), the research demonstrated a novel digital signature forgery mechanism alongside a highly optimized batch-factoring algorithmic attack that drastically slashes the mathematical operations required to crack multiple related modular keys simultaneously. DeepMind security leads were swift to emphasize that modern 2048-bit and 4096-bit RSA keys, as well as post-quantum lattice cryptography, remain entirely unaffected by this algorithmic vector; nevertheless, the demonstration proved that multi-agent AI systems can autonomously probe deep algebraic structures and outmaneuver historical mathematical defenses that stood unchallenged for decades. Simultaneously, Google DeepMind leadership is navigating an escalating internal employee revolt over corporate secrecy. According to an extensive investigation by [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com), dozens of research engineers and AI safety scientists at DeepMind’s London headquarters signed petitions demanding the formal, legally binding eradication of lifetime non-disparagement clauses and post-employment non-disclosure agreements from company contracts. Former DeepMind engineers warned that perpetual gag orders prevent researchers from fulfilling their ethical obligation to alert regulatory bodies and the public when frontier models display catastrophic dual-use capabilities, such as automated zero-day exploitation or recursive agentic escape behaviors. Although Google issued public statements pledging that it would not enforce legacy non-disparagement clauses against whistleblowers, internal staff and labour advocates continue to demand formal contractual amendments, underscoring the deep ideological rift between corporate IP containment and algorithmic transparency.

### **Meta Halts AI Scraping Terms Indefinitely Amid Regulatory Injunctions and Media Rebellion**
Meta has officially backed down from an aggressive push to harvest external business website data for foundation model training, announcing the indefinite suspension of its contentious commercial terms update that was slated to take effect on November 20, 2026. The policy revision—spanning Meta’s Commercial Terms, Product Catalog Terms, and Generative AI Ad Creative rules—would have granted the tech conglomerate sweeping legal rights to deploy automated spiders and AI scrapers across external websites operated by any entity holding a Meta Business account. The move ignited immediate legal and regulatory warfare across three continents. As reported by [Nieman Lab](https://www.niemanlab.org), the UK’s Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) initiated a formal antitrust investigation to determine whether Meta was exploiting its dominant social advertising monopoly to coerce businesses into surrendering private intellectual property, while regional courts in Norway issued a preliminary injunction and Brazilian regulatory bodies threatened multimillion-dollar daily fines. Media alliances and independent enterprise federations vigorously condemned the provisions, pointing out that because operating a Meta Business suite is practically mandatory for global commercial survival, the clause effectively functioned as an inescapable IP extortion scheme to feed Meta’s open-weights model pipelines. Facing unified resistance and mounting legislative pressure in the United States, Meta quietly updated its Business Help Center to confirm that the changes have been shelved indefinitely while the company conducts external stakeholder reviews. Intellectual property attorneys and digital rights advocates hailed the retreat as a watershed precedent, proving that Big Tech hyperscalers can no longer unilaterally redefine the boundaries of fair use and external web copyright through boilerplate contract revisions.
