# Tech Giants Form Open-Weight AI Alliances & Samsung Secures $200B Broadcom Pact

### **Open-Weight AI Coalition Letter and Open Secure AI Alliance Spark Washington Policy Debate**
[Read More](news/The_Open-Weights_Civil_War_Inside_the_Geopolitical_Lobbying_and_Technical_Warfare_Shaping_AI_Sovereignty.md)
On July 24, 2026, a coalition of prominent technology companies and research organizations released an open letter titled "Open Weights and American AI Leadership," urging the U.S. government to refrain from imposing premature restrictions on open-weight artificial intelligence models. The letter argues that open-weight architectures, which developers can download, inspect, and modify, are vital to preventing market monopolization and ensuring national competitiveness. Prominent signatories include NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta, IBM, Dell, Palantir, and Perplexity, with OpenAI and Google joining shortly after publication. Conspicuously absent from the list are Anthropic and Amazon, reflecting a deep commercial and philosophical divide in the industry. Promoting the letter on X.com, NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang made his first-ever post on the platform, stating: "AI will transform every industry, power every company, and be built by every country. Open models strengthen safety and cybersecurity, accelerate innovation and diffusion, and enable sovereignty." The post was quickly quote-posted by Elon Musk, who added, "This has my full support. Jensen is right."

To consolidate this advocacy into concrete action, a subset of 37 companies launched the Open Secure AI Alliance on July 27, 2026. Spearheaded by NVIDIA, the alliance includes Microsoft, Cisco, IBM, the Linux Foundation, Adobe, CrowdStrike, and Salesforce. The coalition’s immediate mission is to develop and share open-source safety, testing, and governance tools. Coinciding with the launch, NVIDIA released its NOOA (NVIDIA-labs Object-Oriented Agents) framework, an open-source tool designed to make autonomous AI agent behaviors easier to trace, test, and audit. The debate in Washington remains intense, as regulators express concern over potential national security risks, such as foreign adversaries utilizing open weights to bypass safety filters or perform distillation attacks. Proponents counter that open-source models allow for faster community-driven vulnerability detection and patch distribution. More information on the alliance's ongoing initiatives can be accessed at [nvidia.com](https://www.nvidia.com).

### **Samsung and Broadcom Finalize $200 Billion Five-Year AI Infrastructure and Foundry Pact**
[Read More](news/Inside_the_200B_Samsung-Broadcom_Alliance_Can_the_One-Stop_Turnkey_Model_Break_TSMC's_Advanced_Packaging_Monopoly.md)
Samsung Electronics and Broadcom announced a massive expansion of their strategic collaboration on July 25, 2026, signing a memorandum of understanding (MOU) valued at more than $200 billion over the next five years. Formally signed at the AI Summit at The Midway in San Francisco, the agreement positions Samsung to supply advanced High Bandwidth Memory (HBM) for Broadcom's next-generation AI accelerators. Furthermore, the partnership marks a major win for Samsung's foundry business, as the company will manufacture Broadcom’s wireless broadband communication and networking silicon using its upcoming 2-nanometer (nm) and sub-2nm process technologies. The deal also covers advanced 2.3D and 2.5D packaging integration, offering a comprehensive "turnkey" solution designed to improve electrical and thermal efficiency.

This strategic partnership is a direct attempt by Samsung to challenge TSMC's dominance in the AI semiconductor and advanced packaging markets. Commenting on the scale of the agreement, Young Hyun Jun, Vice Chairman and CEO of Samsung Electronics' Device Solutions Division, stated: "AI is driving unprecedented demand for tightly integrated semiconductor technologies spanning memory, logic and advanced packaging. By expanding our collaboration with Broadcom across these critical technologies, we look forward to delivering greater value to customers while advancing the AI infrastructure of the future." Charlie Kawwas, President of Broadcom's Semiconductor Solutions Group, echoed this sentiment, noting: "By combining Samsung's memory and foundry expertise with Broadcom's AI and connectivity leadership, we aim to continue to deliver technologies that power the AI infrastructure of the future." Official press details on the deal are available at [samsung.com](https://www.samsung.com).

### **NASA Astronauts and Roscosmos Cosmonauts Return to Earth After 241-Day ISS Mission**
On July 26, 2026, the crew of Expedition 74 safely returned to Earth, concluding a 241-day science mission aboard the International Space Station (ISS). The Soyuz MS-28 spacecraft carrying NASA astronaut Chris Williams and Roscosmos cosmonauts Sergey Kud-Sverchkov and Sergei Mikaev undocked from the orbital laboratory at 3:03 a.m. EDT. Following a deorbit burn, the capsule executed a successful parachute-assisted landing on the steppe of Kazakhstan, southeast of the town of Dzhezkazgan, at 6:27 a.m. EDT (3:27 p.m. local time). 

The mission, which launched on November 27, 2025, saw the crew complete 3,856 orbits of Earth and travel approximately 102 million miles. This flight marked the first spaceflight for both Williams and Mikaev, and the second for Kud-Sverchkov. The crew conducted numerous experiments in microgravity, including studies on human physiology, space biology, and physical science. Prior to their undocking and departure, command of the International Space Station was formally handed over to NASA astronaut Jessica Meir during a traditional change-of-command ceremony. Updates regarding the post-landing recovery process and medical evaluations are posted at [nasa.gov](https://www.nasa.gov) and [space.com](https://www.space.com).

### **Fujitsu and Japanese Robotics Giants Partner with NVIDIA to Accelerate Physical AI**
[Read More](news/The_Japanese_Robotics_Rebellion_Inside_Fujitsu’s_Kozuchi_OS,_NVIDIA_Cosmos,_and_the_Quest_for_Sovereign_Physical_AI.md)
Fujitsu announced a major industrial partnership on July 24, 2026, joining forces with Japan’s leading robotics manufacturers—FANUC, Yaskawa Electric, and Kawasaki Heavy Industries—to accelerate the development and implementation of Physical AI. The collaborative initiative integrates NVIDIA's robotics software stack, including the Omniverse simulation platform, Isaac robotics platform, and Cosmos foundation models, to bridge the digital and physical realms. The core software engine of this partnership is the "Fujitsu Kozuchi Physical OS," an open, sovereign control platform designed to govern robot task planning, collaborative control, and real-time business application integration. The coalition plans to deploy these Physical AI systems across three primary sectors: manufacturing (optimizing assembly line tasks), logistics (automating material handling), and healthcare (assisting with medical specimen transport).

The creation of the coalition follows a visit to Tokyo by NVIDIA CEO Jensen Huang in mid-July, during which he emphasized that Japan's deep historical expertise in mechatronics and manufacturing makes it the ideal global epicentre for the "next industrial revolution" powered by physical robotics AI. The participating companies have officially joined the NVIDIA-led Cosmos Coalition, which includes other major Japanese industrial leaders such as Toyota, Hitachi, and NEC. Fujitsu plans to conduct proof-of-concept testing for industrial robot control at its own factory by the end of September 2026, aiming to make the Kozuchi platform available to commercial partners by the end of the year. Detailed announcements regarding the Kozuchi OS can be found at [global.fujitsu](https://global.fujitsu).

### **XTEND Integrates Atlas Robotics and Accelerates $1.5 Billion NYSE Merger**
XTEND, a developer of AI-driven autonomous operating systems for drones and robots, announced on July 27, 2026, that it has completed the operational integration of Atlas Aerospace, a Latvia-based robotic aircraft manufacturer. The integration consolidates XTEND's hardware and software capabilities, combining its XOS operating system with Atlas's advanced micro-UAV systems. This operational milestone comes amid progress toward XTEND's pending merger with JFB Construction Holdings, a transaction valued at $1.5 billion. The companies filed their second amended registration statement on Form S-4 with the SEC on July 20, 2026, with the transaction expected to close in the third quarter of 2026.

Upon closing, the combined company will be renamed XTEND AI Robotics. According to an amendment to the merger agreement executed on July 16, 2026, the companies have shifted their planned stock exchange listing from Nasdaq to the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE), where the combined entity will trade under the ticker symbol "XTND." The amendment also extended the outside closing date to October 31, 2026, and modified the financial terms, setting a minimum closing cash requirement of $60 million. The merger aims to establish the newly formed XTEND AI Robotics as a major provider of autonomous systems for the defense, public safety, and industrial inspection markets.

### **FDA Approves Zidesamtinib as Next-Generation Target for Pre-Treated ROS1-Positive Lung Cancer**
[Read More](news/Inside_the_10.6B_Molecular_Chessboard_How_GSK’s_Newly_Approved_Jideytro_(Zidesamtinib)_Rewrites_the_Code_of_Kinase_Resistance.md)
On July 22, 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) granted accelerated approval to zidesamtinib (brand name Jideytro) for the treatment of adult patients with locally advanced or metastatic ROS1-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC) who have previously received at least one ROS1 tyrosine kinase inhibitor (TKI). The approval was granted nearly two months ahead of the scheduled PDUFA target action date of September 18, 2026. Zidesamtinib, developed by GSK, is a next-generation, brain-penetrant, ROS1-selective inhibitor designed to remain active against a broad spectrum of ROS1 resistance mutations.

The FDA's decision was based on clinical efficacy data from the global, open-label, single-arm Phase 1/2 ARROS-1 trial. In the pre-treated patient cohort, zidesamtinib achieved an objective response rate (ORR) of 44%, demonstrating durable responses in patients with central nervous system (CNS) metastases and those harboring the G2032R solvent front resistance mutation, which typically renders earlier-generation TKIs ineffective. The drug's safety profile showed that the most common adverse reactions (occurring in 15% or more of patients) included edema, peripheral neuropathy, constipation, fatigue, and dyspnea. The approval represents a crucial therapeutic advancement for NSCLC patients who have developed resistance to initial targeted therapies. The official approval statement can be found on [fda.gov](https://www.fda.gov).
