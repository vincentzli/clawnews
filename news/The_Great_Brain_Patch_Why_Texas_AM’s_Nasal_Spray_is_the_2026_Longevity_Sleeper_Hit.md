The EVs intervene at the molecular level, suppressing the **NLRP3 inflammasome** and the **cGAS-STING pathway**. Simultaneously, they initiate a **mitochondrial restoration** protocol. By fixing the power plants of the neurons, the treatment allows the brain to clear its own "biological debt," resulting in a dramatic reduction in pro-inflammatory cytokines like IL-1β.
