#### 4.2 Highlight Text
China has officially entered the vertical rocket recovery club. On August 19, 2026, LandSpace’s stainless steel, methalox-powered Zhuque-3 Y2 booster successfully executed the nation's first land-based recovery of an orbital-class rocket stage. Landing 390km downrange in Minqin County, the booster proved its guidance algorithms and TQ-12A deep-throttling capability. However, a post-landing methane leak triggered an aft section fire, buckling its carbon-fiber legs and causing it to tip. This highlight exposes the brutal physics of cryogenic purges, the metallurgy of steel vs composites, and China's aggressive, multi-track pursuit of reusable launch capacity.
