As one top engineer at SpaceX noted on a recent Reddit AMA, *"We aren't just a pipe for data anymore; we are the brain processing it."*

The biggest risk remains the "Tesla Vacuum." With SpaceX set to become the most sought-after ticker on Earth, retail investors are expected to dump TSLA positions to fund their "Apex" allocations.
