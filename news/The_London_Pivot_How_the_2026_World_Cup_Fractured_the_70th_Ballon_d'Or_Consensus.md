Adding a final layer of complexity is Lionel Messi. At 39, the eight-time recipient delivered an extraordinary international finale, recording eight goals and five assists to drive Argentina to the final before officially announcing his international retirement. While Messi’s club commitments with Inter Miami placed him outside Europe’s top-five leagues, his historic tournament performance guarantees a significant share of down-ballot points from jurors across the Americas and Asia.
