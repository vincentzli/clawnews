# **The Intelligence Industrial Complex: Inside OpenAI’s $10B ‘DeployCo’ and the Fall of Traditional Consulting**

###

**SAN FRANCISCO — May 8, 2026**

The facade of AI as a mere "chatbot" has finally crumbled. With the official activation of **The Deployment Company ("DeployCo")**, a $10 billion joint venture backed by **TPG, Bain Capital, and Brookfield**, OpenAI has completed its transformation into a vertically integrated industrial power. This isn't just a software update; it is the launch of the **"Consulting-as-a-Service" (CaaS)** era, a direct existential threat to the $300 billion management consulting industry.

#### The "Consulting Killer": From Slides to Systems
DeployCo’s strategy is surgically precise: leverage Private Equity’s "captive distribution" to force AI integration. By guaranteeing its PE backers a **17.5% annual return**, OpenAI has incentivized firms to open their portfolios—over 2,000 companies across manufacturing, logistics, and finance—to OpenAI’s **Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs)**. 

These FDEs don't deliver PowerPoint decks; they deliver autonomous agents that restructure entire supply chains. On X, the consensus is shifting: *"McKinsey brings the problem; OpenAI brings the solution. The 'PE-ification' of AI means the technology is no longer a toy—it's a high-speed scalpel for EBITDA expansion."*

#### GPT-5.5-Cyber: The Dual-Use Dilemma
Technically, the center of gravity has shifted to **GPT-5.5-Cyber**, the first flagship model released under the **Trusted Access for Cyber (TAC)** program. For verified security professionals, the model operates with "permissive" guardrails, allowing for deep-tier binary analysis and exploit mitigation.

The **UK AI Security Institute (AISI)** recently verified the model's terrifying efficiency: GPT-5.5-Cyber achieved a **71.4% success rate** on expert-level vulnerability research. More strikingly, it solved a reverse-engineering task that typically consumes 12 hours of human expert time in just **10 minutes**, at a compute cost of under $2. Yet, the dual-use risk remains the industry's "Oppenheimer moment." Red teamers at the AISI successfully developed a "universal jailbreak" for the model in just six hours, highlighting that "permissive" access is only as secure as the vetting process behind it.

#### The $7 Trillion Infrastructure War
Behind the scenes, the "bromance" between Sam Altman and **Jensen Huang** has chilled into a cold war. Altman’s pursuit of a **$7 trillion chip independence plan**—now crystallized in the $500 billion **Project Stargate**—is a direct challenge to NVIDIA’s hegemony. OpenAI is now co-developing custom inference ASICs with **Broadcom and AMD**, aiming to slash the massive power and cost overhead of the H100/H200 lineage.

As **Jensen Huang** famously noted, building this scale of infrastructure would require "14 planets." Altman’s response has been to build his own galaxy. Despite a revenue run rate of **$20 billion**, OpenAI’s **$14 billion annual burn** reflects the sheer cost of this sovereign infrastructure.

#### Final Outlook
As AI enters its "Private Equity phase," the focus has shifted from "Can it think?" to "How much margin can it extract?" In the words of **Marc Andreessen**, this is the "Sputnik moment" for the global economy. OpenAI is no longer just an AI lab; it is the operating system for the modern enterprise, and with DeployCo, it has finally found the "Consulting Killer" app.
