The Iron Gridlock: Inside the Stalled Gaza Peace Plan and the Creeping Borders of the Yellow Line

1.2 Body
When the ceasefire between Israel and Hamas was signed on October 9, 2025, it was hailed by the incoming Trump administration as the cornerstone of a new Middle Eastern architecture. The centerpiece of the 20-point peace plan was the establishment of the Board of Peace (BoP), an international transition authority formally chartered in January 2026 at the World Economic Forum in Davos and endorsed by United Nations Security Council Resolution 2803, passed in late 2026. The plan established the "Yellow Line," a temporary military demarcation line that initially left the Israel Defense Forces (IDF) in control of 53% of the Gaza Strip, with the remaining 47% designated for Palestinian civil administration. 

Ten months later, that architecture is fracturing under the weight of active military escalation and a fundamental deadlock over the sequencing of disarmament.
