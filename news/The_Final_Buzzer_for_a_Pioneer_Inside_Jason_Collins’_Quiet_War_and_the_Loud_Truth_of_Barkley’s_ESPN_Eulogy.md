Collins had passed away 24 hours earlier at the age of 47. "We live in a homophobic society," Barkley stated during the Western Conference Semifinals coverage. "Anybody who thinks we ain't got a bunch of gay players in all sports, they're just stupid. They’re in the NFL, they’re in the MLB, and they’re in this league. And the fact they still feel they gotta hide is on us."

This "Trojan horse" protocol, which delivers chemotherapy directly to glioblastoma proteins, initially yielded tumor shrinkage. However, the aggressive nature of the cancer returned in early May. NBA Commissioner Adam Silver noted in a statement that Collins died after a "valiant eight-month fight."
