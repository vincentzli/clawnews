### **Ultrasound Olfaction: BCI Breakthrough Enables "Virtual Scent"**
Researchers have successfully demonstrated the ability to induce specific olfactory sensations—smells—using targeted ultrasound waves to stimulate the brain's olfactory bulb. Unlike previous "digital scent" attempts that required chemical cartridges, this non-invasive BCI (Brain-Computer Interface) technology uses the "Ultrasound Focal Point" method. Users in the pilot study reported smelling roses, coffee, and even rain while wearing a specialized headset. This technology is being touted as the "Missing Piece" for full-immersion VR and a potential breakthrough for patients who lost their sense of smell due to viral infections.
