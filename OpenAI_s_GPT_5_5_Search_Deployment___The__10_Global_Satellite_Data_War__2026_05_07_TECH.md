### **Bio-Synthetic Liver Breakthrough: Zurich’s 3D-Printed Success** [Read More](news/The_Zurich_Protocol_How_Bio-Synthetic_Livers_Just_Upgraded_the_Human_Hardware.md)
Researchers at the University Hospital Zurich have announced the first successful long-term survival of a patient with a "100% Bio-Synthetic" 3D-printed liver. The organ was constructed using the patient’s own induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) seeded onto a decellularized porcine scaffold, a technique that eliminates the need for immunosuppressant drugs. The patient, a 45-year-old male with end-stage cirrhosis, has shown "normal metabolic function" 90 days post-transplant. This breakthrough addresses the global donor shortage, which currently sees 20% of patients on transplant lists die before an organ becomes available. "We have moved from organ harvesting to organ manufacturing," lead surgeon Dr. Pierre-Alain Clavien shared on X. The medical community on Reddit is now debating the "ethics of longevity," as bio-synthetic organs could potentially extend human lifespans significantly, raising questions about healthcare equity and the "Ship of Theseus" problem of human identity.
